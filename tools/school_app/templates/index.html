What the statuses mean

Free — the section timetable says this period is free and the student's own matched group schedule does not show a lesson. Individual group schedules take precedence over a section-level free cell.
In class — the section is timetabled at this moment.
Unknown — no schedule covers this student, so no claim is made. This is an answer, not an error: KG1, KG2 and Grade 1 have rosters and subject enrolment but appear in no timetable.
Confidence on an individual — high: their own group was matched to a lesson; medium: matched less firmly; low: their section is timetabled but their specific group could not be identified, so the subject shown is the section's, not certainly theirs.
