# ISC-Sharjah Campus Facilities

## Sports Facilities
- ISC-Sharjah has two semi-Olympic-sized swimming pools. Only one of the two is currently in service; the second pool is marked "Not In Use".
- ISC-Sharjah has an all-season running track and a large sports field.
- ISC-Sharjah has two large indoor sports halls: the Main Gymnasium and the Old Gym, which serves as a secondary sports hall.
- ISC-Sharjah has a dedicated Kindergarten gymnasium, separate from the main sports halls.
- ISC-Sharjah has one indoor basketball court, inside the gymnasium block, and outdoor basketball courts on the campus grounds.
- ISC-Sharjah has a gymnastic hall on the First Floor.

## ITL Hall
The ITL Hall (SABIS Integrated Testing and Learning Hall) at ISC-Sharjah has 580 computers. Students use it for examinations and to revise and review material they have learned.

## Classrooms
All main classrooms for Grades 1 through 12 at ISC-Sharjah are equipped with Interactive Smart Boards.

## Science, Art, Music and Technology Rooms
- ISC-Sharjah has three science laboratories: biology, chemistry, and physics.
- ISC-Sharjah has two computer science rooms for Grades 1 to 12, plus a separate Kindergarten computer lab.
- ISC-Sharjah has one art room.
- ISC-Sharjah has one music room, which serves Grades 5 to 8.

## Kindergarten Building
Kindergarten students at ISC-Sharjah have their own independent building with colourful hallways, engaging classrooms, a large playground, and a car track.

## School Theater
The ISC-Sharjah school theater has professional-quality sound and lighting systems and is used for music productions, presentations, and assemblies.

## Library
The ISC-Sharjah library serves the school community. It has a suite of computers for online research and a wide range of fiction and non-fiction books in English, Arabic, and French. The library is on the First Floor and is used primarily by Grades 9 to 12.

## Cafeteria
The ISC-Sharjah cafeteria is a bright, modern space where students can eat food brought from home or buy wholesome sandwiches and fresh fruit at break time or after school.

## Medical Clinics
ISC-Sharjah has two clinics, one for boys and one for girls, with two medical doctors on site during school hours.

## Campus Location
ISC-Sharjah is situated on a spacious campus on University City Road, Industrial Area 6, Sharjah, UAE.
