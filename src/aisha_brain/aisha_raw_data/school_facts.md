# ISC-Sharjah School Facts

## School Identity and History
- ISC-Sharjah was founded in 1976 and was the first SABIS school in the UAE.
- ISC-Sharjah has an enrollment of more than 4,000 students from over 64 different nationalities.
- The Director of ISC-Sharjah is Mr. Wissam Malaeb.
- ISC-Sharjah is a private, non-selective, independent, English-medium day school.
- The SABIS Network, of which ISC-Sharjah is part, was founded in 1886.
- ISC-Sharjah is accredited by the UAE government and by international accreditation agencies.

## Curriculum and Academic Levels
- ISC-Sharjah implements the SABIS Educational System, which draws on the American and British educational systems and also delivers the subjects required by the UAE Ministry of Education (Arabic, Islamic Studies / Religion, and Social Studies).
- ISC-Sharjah students can sit for SAT, TOEFL and AP examinations on the American pathway.
- ISC-Sharjah students can sit for IGCSE, O Level, AS Level and A Level examinations on the British pathway.
- ISC-Sharjah teaches Pre-Kindergarten and Kindergarten (Pre-KG, KG 1 and KG 2).
- ISC-Sharjah Lower School covers Grades 1 to 5.
- ISC-Sharjah Middle School covers Grades 6 to 8.
- ISC-Sharjah High School covers Grades 9 to 12.

## What Time the School Day Starts at ISC-Sharjah
- The ISC-Sharjah school day starts at around 7:55 AM, when the first bell rings and the National Anthem is played. Students are expected to be in the playground before that time.
- A student who arrives at ISC-Sharjah after the first bell at around 7:55 AM requires a Late Slip from a Supervisor.
- ISC-Sharjah bell times, period start and end times, break times and dismissal times, other than the 7:55 AM start, are not held in this knowledge base. For these, contact the ISC-Sharjah school office on +971 6 558 2211.

## Admissions Summary
- ISC-Sharjah has no application deadline and accepts applications throughout the year based on space availability.
- ISC-Sharjah charges no joining fee.
- To apply to ISC-Sharjah you can complete the Application Form on the school website, or apply in person at the school reception desk.
- To book a campus tour of ISC-Sharjah, contact the school directly or complete the Interest Form on the school website.

## Sports Facilities
- ISC-Sharjah has two semi-Olympic-sized swimming pools. Only one of the two is currently in service; the second pool is marked "Not In Use".
- ISC-Sharjah has an all-season running track and a large sports field.
- ISC-Sharjah has two large indoor sports halls: the Main Gymnasium and the Old Gym, which serves as a secondary sports hall.
- ISC-Sharjah has a dedicated Kindergarten gymnasium, separate from the main sports halls.
- ISC-Sharjah has one indoor basketball court, inside the gymnasium block, and outdoor basketball courts on the campus grounds.
- ISC-Sharjah has a gymnastic hall on the First Floor.

## Academic Facilities
- The ITL Hall (SABIS Integrated Testing and Learning Hall) at ISC-Sharjah has 580 computers. Students use it for examinations and to revise and review material.
- All main classrooms for Grades 1 through 12 at ISC-Sharjah are equipped with Interactive Smart Boards.
- ISC-Sharjah has three science laboratories: biology, chemistry, and physics.
- ISC-Sharjah has two computer science rooms for Grades 1 to 12, plus a separate Kindergarten computer lab.
- ISC-Sharjah has one art room and one music room, which serves Grades 5 to 8.
- The ISC-Sharjah library has a suite of computers for online research and a wide range of fiction and non-fiction books in English, Arabic, and French.
- The ISC-Sharjah school theater has professional-quality sound and lighting systems.

## Campus and Student Services
- The ISC-Sharjah kindergarten has its own independent building with a large playground and a car track.
- ISC-Sharjah has two medical doctors on site during school hours, in two clinics: a boys' clinic and a girls' clinic.
- The ISC-Sharjah cafeteria sells wholesome sandwiches and fresh fruit. Students may also bring food from home.
- ISC-Sharjah has a bookshop, a uniform shop, and a lost-and-found office.
- ISC-Sharjah staff parking holds 150 vehicles.

## SLO — Student Life Organization
- SLO stands for Student Life Organization, also known as the SABIS Student Life Organization.
- The SLO is a student-run organization that exists in every SABIS school, including ISC-Sharjah.
- The ISC-Sharjah SLO departments are: Academic, Discipline, Sports, Arts, Community Service, Environment, Media, and Social.
- The Academic Department of the SLO helps students who are struggling with their studies by organizing peer tutoring and study groups.
- The Discipline Department of the SLO helps maintain order and a positive school environment.
- The Sports Department of the SLO organizes inter-school competitions and sports events.
- A prefect is a student leader in the SLO. Prefects are elected or appointed students who help organize school activities, maintain discipline, and act as role models.
- Prefects wear a special badge or uniform so they can be identified as student leaders.
- Any ISC-Sharjah student can take part in SLO activities and apply to become a prefect.
- The SLO gives students the opportunity to gain and refine life skills and to take responsibility for their school community.
