# ISC-Sharjah Campus Map

## How the Campus Is Laid Out
The ISC-Sharjah campus has two floors. The Ground Floor is divided into Zones 1 to 5 and holds the Kindergarten wing, the gymnasium and swimming pool, the main administration block, the cafeteria, the science laboratories and the lower-grade classrooms. The First Floor is Zone 6 and holds the library, the multi-purpose hall, the upper-grade classrooms and the staff offices. The main entrance, the atrium and the reception are on the Ground Floor.

## Ground Floor Zone Index
- Zone 1 of the ISC-Sharjah Ground Floor is the Kindergarten wing.
- Zone 2 of the ISC-Sharjah Ground Floor is the main administration block, including the atrium, main entrance, reception, principal's office, vice-principal's office, conference room, IT and control rooms, server room, and the secretary, operator and coordinator offices.
- Zone 3 of the ISC-Sharjah Ground Floor is the cafeteria block, including the kitchen and stores, the bookshop, the uniform shop, lost and found, the staff lounge and the locker rooms.
- Zone 4 of the ISC-Sharjah Ground Floor is the gymnasium and swimming pool block, including the changing rooms, showers, the indoor basketball court, the Grades 5 to 8 music room, the stage and the Old Gym.
- Zone 5 of the ISC-Sharjah Ground Floor is the science and senior block, including the biology, chemistry and physics laboratories, the art room, the video conference room, Grade 6 classrooms E to K, Grade 10 classrooms L, SA and SB, and all Grade 11 classrooms.

## First Floor Zone Index
- Zone 6 is the whole ISC-Sharjah First Floor. It holds the library, the multi-purpose hall, upper-grade classrooms, the HR offices, the AQC Coordinator's office, the university advisor's office, the recruitment department, the social workers' office, the Grades 3 to 4 science room, the prayer room, the gymnastic hall and the swimming pool deck.

## Finding Your Way Around ISC-Sharjah
- The ISC-Sharjah Main Entrance, Atrium, Reception, Principal's Office, cafeteria, gymnasium, swimming pool and science laboratories are all on the Ground Floor.
- The ISC-Sharjah Library, Multi-Purpose Hall and University Advisor's Office are all on the First Floor.
- Kindergarten, Grade 1 and Grade 2 at ISC-Sharjah are entirely on the Ground Floor.
- Grades 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 12 at ISC-Sharjah are entirely on the First Floor in Zone 6.
- Grade 11 at ISC-Sharjah is entirely on the Ground Floor in Zone 5.
- Grades 3, 6 and 10 at ISC-Sharjah are each split between the Ground Floor and the First Floor, so the classroom letter matters when giving directions.
- When giving the location of a room at ISC-Sharjah, state the floor, the zone and the nearest landmark.
