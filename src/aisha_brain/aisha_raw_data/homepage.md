# ISC-Sharjah — Overview

## About the School
The International School of Choueifat — Sharjah (ISC-Sharjah) opened in 1976 and was the first school to bring the SABIS Educational System to the UAE. It is a private, non-selective, independent, English-medium day school. ISC-Sharjah has an enrollment of more than 4,000 students from over 64 different nationalities.

## Curriculum
ISC-Sharjah implements the SABIS Educational System, which draws on the American and British educational systems and also delivers the subjects required by the UAE Ministry of Education (Arabic, Islamic Studies / Religion, and Social Studies). Students can sit for external examinations on either pathway:
- American pathway: SAT, TOEFL, and AP examinations.
- British pathway: IGCSE, O Level, AS Level, and A Level examinations.

## Academic Levels
- Pre-Kindergarten and Kindergarten: Pre-KG, KG 1 and KG 2.
- Lower School: Grades 1 to 5.
- Middle School: Grades 6 to 8.
- High School: Grades 9 to 12.
